## The full prompt

Replace `[YOUR PET]` with your animal and `[YOUR LOGO]` with your cafe's name or logo. In TalkPix the pet photo and the logo are attached as reference images, which is what keeps the animal identical; with text alone, a model will invent a dog of its own.

```text
Photoreal 30-second vertical smartphone video, 9:16, one handheld take from the customer's side of the bar of a small modern specialty cafe: the barista is [YOUR PET], exactly the animal in its photos, the same species, breed, size, fur colour, markings and face in every second; a dog stays a dog and a cat stays a cat; one head, four legs, real paws and a real tail, never human arms or hands, and it never talks. It wears a small dark apron.

The place: a bright modern cafe with a white stone counter, a white espresso machine with a steam wand, a grinder, shelves of cups, warm daylight. The cafe is branded with [YOUR LOGO]: a backlit sign behind the bar carries it, a small print of it is on the paper cups, and the logo's colour is the cafe's accent colour on the sign and a few cups. The sign is only glimpsed behind the pet; nothing else carries any other lettering.

Props, the same in every second: a black card terminal, a glass espresso cup, a steel milk pitcher, a white one-litre milk carton with [YOUR LOGO] printed large on its side, and a brown paper takeaway cup with a white rim.

0-3s: the pet stands on its hind legs behind the counter facing the camera and lifts the card terminal with both front paws, holding it out toward the customer; a hand reaches in from the bottom of the frame and taps a bank card on it; a short beep, a green tick on the screen, payment taken. The pet gives a small nod.

3-7s: the pet turns to the espresso machine beside it, pushes the empty glass cup under the group head with a paw, and presses the button with its paw; the machine starts and dark espresso begins to pour into the glass.

7-9s: while the espresso drips, the pet turns its head over its shoulder toward the customer and gives a small, knowing smile, then turns back.

9-16s: the pet lifts the milk carton with both front paws, the printed [YOUR LOGO] clearly on its side, and pours a long stream of milk from high above into the steel pitcher on the counter; as it pours, the pet lowers its head into the falling stream and laps at the milk with its tongue, milk still going into the pitcher, and while lapping it looks straight into the camera; then it lifts the carton away, a little milk on its whiskers.

16-20s: the pet carries the pitcher to the steam wand, tilts it under the wand and steams the milk, steam rising, until it is glossy foam.

20-24s: the pet pours the espresso from the glass into the brown paper cup, then pours the steamed milk on top in one slow stream that draws a small rosetta in the foam.

24-30s: the pet lifts the full cup in both front paws and, before anyone can take it, leans in and sniffs the foam on its way up, its nose dipping into it; it then holds the cup out toward the customer with a small blob of white foam on the tip of its nose, looks into the camera and gives one last smile; only in the last second does the hand reach in and take the cup.

Rules: one pet, the same in every second, no second animal; the same props throughout, none vanishing or changing; of the customer only one hand is ever seen; the only lettering anywhere is [YOUR LOGO], on the carton, the sign and the cup; no captions or subtitles.

Priority: 1 the pet's identity, 2 the lapping at the milk stream while looking into the lens, 3 the foam left on the nose, 4 the logo readable on the carton, 5 believable animal movement with real paws.

Sound: cafe room tone, the terminal's beep, the espresso machine, the milk pouring, lapping, the hiss of the steam wand, the cup set down; no music.
```

## Why it holds together for 30 seconds

Long AI videos usually fall apart in the same ways: the animal turns into a different breed, props appear and vanish, and stray text shows up on every surface. Most of this prompt is there to stop those three things.

1. **Identity first, in so many words.** "The same species, breed, size, fur colour, markings and face in every second" and "a dog stays a dog" leave no room for drift. "Never human arms or hands" stops the model from giving the pet hands to hold the carton.
2. **A fixed prop list.** Every object is named once, up front, as "the same in every second". Objects that are only mentioned in the timeline tend to change shape or disappear.
3. **One hand of the customer, never more.** A second person in frame is a second identity to keep stable. A single hand at the edge of the frame is enough to sell the payment and the handover.
4. **The only lettering is the logo.** Without this rule, models print gibberish captions, menus and labels all over the scene.
5. **A timeline in seconds.** Each beat has a time window, so the model doesn't rush the ending or skip the milk pour.
6. **A priority list.** When the model can't satisfy everything, it knows what to keep: the pet's identity first, then the lapping shot, then the foam on the nose.
7. **Sound written out.** The terminal beep, the espresso machine and the steam wand are named, and music is ruled out, so the audio matches what you see.

## Photos that work for pet videos

- **Just the pet, face and body visible.** No people in the shot, no other animals.
- **A second photo from another angle** helps the model keep markings exact, especially for patterned coats.
- **Sharp and well lit.** A blurry or dark photo gives the model room to invent fur and eye colour.
- **Say it is AI-made when you post it.** Most platforms have an AI label; use it.
